Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026. How we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Noumeer Delights ("we," "our," or "us"). We are a home-based cloud kitchen operating from Byculla (West), Mumbai. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website. By using our site, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Information: Your name, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order or contact us.
  • Order Information: Items selected, quantities, special requests, and estimated totals.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and visit timestamps — collected automatically.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: Cookies, web beacons, and pixel tags used for analytics and advertising (see Section 7).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your food orders via WhatsApp
  • Communicate with you about order status and updates
  • Improve and optimize our website and user experience
  • Analyze traffic and usage patterns via Google Analytics
  • Serve relevant targeted advertisements via Google AdSense
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Google AdSense — Advertising Disclosure

This site participates in Google AdSense, which may display personalized or non-personalized ads based on your browsing behavior.

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites across the internet. These cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads tailored to your interests.

You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by:

  • Visiting Google Ads Settings
  • Visiting Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out
  • Using your browser's cookie controls to block third-party cookies

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects anonymous information including pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, and geographic region. No personally identifiable information is linked to this data.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For more information, see Google's partner site policies.

6. Cookie Policy

6.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and provide analytical and advertising insights.

6.2 Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality, such as remembering your dark/light theme preference.
  • Analytics Cookies: Set by Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics (e.g., pages viewed, session duration).
  • Advertising Cookies: Set by Google AdSense to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. These may track your activity across multiple websites.
  • Consent Cookie: Set by us to remember your cookie preference (accepted or declined).

6.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality. See aboutcookies.org for browser-specific instructions.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website links to and integrates with third-party services including:

  • WhatsApp: Used for order communication. Your order details are shared with us via WhatsApp's platform.
  • Instagram: Links to our @noumeerdelights profile. Instagram's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Fonts & CDN libraries: Used for typography and icons; these may log requests to Google's servers.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take prompt action to remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
